## Entry Format

```
| [ID] | [Name] | [Purpose] | [Inputs] | [Outputs] | [Safety] | [Status] |
```

## Safety Levels

- HIGH - Requires approval
- MEDIUM - Standard care
- LOW - Minimal risk

## Status Labels

- ACTIVE - Currently available
- INACTIVE - Not currently used
- ARCHIVED - Historical reference
- PLANNED - To be created
